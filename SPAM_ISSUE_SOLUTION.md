# Email Spam Issue - Current Status & Solutions

## ⚠️ Current Problem

**Both contact forms are sending emails that go to spam:**
- Contact Page form ❌ → Spam folder
- Home Page Quick Enquiry form ❌ → Spam folder

**Root Cause:** EmailJS uses Gmail API, which doesn't have proper email authentication (SPF/DKIM) for custom domains. This causes spam filters to flag these emails.

## 🔧 Solution Options

### Option 1: Short-Term Fix (Manual)

**For the recipient (support@lordvservices.com):**

1. **Mark emails as "Not Spam"**
   - Open email in spam folder
   - Click "Not Spam" button
   - Future emails will go to inbox

2. **Add sender to contacts**
   - Open the spam email
   - Click on sender email
   - Click "Add to Contacts"
   - Future emails will be trusted

3. **Create a Gmail filter**
   - Open the spam email
   - Click three dots → "Filter messages like this"
   - Create filter with "Never send to Spam"
   - All future emails will go to inbox

---

### Option 2: Long-Term Fix (Recommended)

**Set up proper email authentication:**

This requires domain access and DNS configuration.

#### Steps:

1. **Add SPF record** to your domain:
   ```
   TXT record: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
   ```

2. **Add DKIM records** to your domain:
   - Generated by your email provider
   - Usually 2-3 TXT records

3. **Add DMARC policy:**
   ```
   TXT record: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:admin@lordvservices.com
   ```

**This is a one-time setup** that will prevent all emails from going to spam!

---

### Option 3: Alternative Email Service

**Switch to an email service with better deliverability:**

1. **SendGrid** - Has built-in authentication
2. **Mailgun** - Better spam avoidance
3. **AWS SES** - Enterprise-level deliverability
4. **Resend** - Modern email API with great deliverability

These services require more setup but have much better spam avoidance.
